## 3. Sender Policy Framework (SPF)

### Record Specification
- **Host**: `spectralabs.io`
- **Type**: `TXT`
- **TTL**: `3600`
- **Value**:
  ```text
  v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all
  ```

### Audit & Validation Checklist
1. **Single Record Rule (RFC 7208 §3.2)**:
   - Exactly **one** SPF TXT record exists on the apex domain `spectralabs.io`.
   - Verified via `dig TXT spectralabs.io` / `Resolve-DnsName -Name spectralabs.io -Type TXT`: multiple TXT records exist for domain ownership verification (`google-site-verification`), but only a single TXT record starts with `v=spf1`.
2. **Sending Service Coverage**:
   - `include:_spf.google.com` comprehensively covers all authorized Google Workspace outbound relay IPs (IPv4 and IPv6).
   - No unauthorized or third-party relays are permitted to originate mail on behalf of `spectralabs.io`.
3. **DNS Lookup Limit (RFC 7208 §4.6.4)**:
   - The SPF specification enforces a strict limit of **10 DNS lookups** per evaluation to prevent denial-of-service amplification attacks.
   - **Lookup Breakdown**:
     1. Initial query: `spectralabs.io` -> `include:_spf.google.com` (1 lookup)
     2. Nested lookup: `_spf.google.com` contains `include:_netblocks.google.com`, `include:_netblocks2.google.com`, `include:_netblocks3.google.com` (3 lookups)
     3. Total mechanism lookups: **4 lookups**.
     4. **Result**: 4 / 10 lookups utilized (6 lookup headroom remaining; 100% compliant).
4. **Qualifier Strategy**:
   - Initial state: `~all` (SoftFail) to enable delivery monitoring alongside DMARC reporting.
   - Target production state: `-all` (Fail / Hard Reject) after 1 week of clean DMARC aggregate reports.

## 4. DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM)

### Record Specification
- **Selector**: `google`
- **Host**: `google._domainkey.spectralabs.io`
- **Type**: `TXT`
- **TTL**: `3600`
- **Value**:
  ```text
  v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=MIIBIjANBgkqhkiG9w0BAQEFAAOCAQ8AMIIBCgKCAQEA0wMh1i+kF4u5bC7X3T3oG6vP6u+z5y9q0Zk+A7j9N2l3kX...
  ```

### Audit & Validation Checklist
1. **Selector Check**:
   - Primary selector: `google` (matches Google Workspace admin console export and outbound signature headers).
2. **Key Length & Algorithm**:
   - Algorithm: RSA (`k=rsa`).
   - Key Length: **2048-bit RSA** (1024-bit is deprecated by NIST and RFC 8301; 2048-bit provides robust protection against factorization).
3. **Alignment**:
   - In outbound mail headers generated by `email.ts`, the `DKIM-Signature` header specifies `d=spectralabs.io` and `s=google`, matching the `From: ...@spectralabs.io` address. This guarantees DKIM relaxed (`adkim=r`) and strict (`adkim=s`) alignment.

## 5. DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting, and Conformance)

### Record Specification
- **Host**: `_dmarc.spectralabs.io`
- **Type**: `TXT`
- **TTL**: `3600`
- **Value**:
  ```text
  v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc-reports@spectralabs.io; ruf=mailto:dmarc-forensics@spectralabs.io; sp=none; fo=1; pct=100
  ```

### Audit & Validation Checklist
1. **Reporting Destination (`rua`)**:
   - Aggregate reporting URI: `rua=mailto:dmarc-reports@spectralabs.io`
   - Failure / forensic reporting URI: `ruf=mailto:dmarc-forensics@spectralabs.io`
   - Daily aggregate XML feedback reports are digested by the security operations inbox to monitor spoofing attempts and legitimate delivery rates.
2. **Alignment & Failure Reporting**:
   - `fo=1`: Generates failure reports if either SPF or DKIM fails alignment.
   - `pct=100`: Policy applies to 100% of outbound messages.
3. **DMARC Rollout Policy (Decision 2)**:
   - **Week 1 (Current)**: `p=none` (Monitoring mode). All mail is delivered; aggregate XML reports monitor for delivery issues or misconfigured senders without risking rejection of legitimate mail.
   - **Week 2**: `p=quarantine; pct=100`. Unauthenticated/spoofed messages are redirected to recipient spam/quarantine folders.
   - **Week 3+ (Final)**: `p=reject; pct=100`. All unaligned and unauthorized mail claiming to be from `spectralabs.io` is rejected outright by receiving MTAs.
